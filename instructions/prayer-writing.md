# Prayer Writing — Too Raw Media

Write original, heartfelt Christian prayers that sound natural when spoken aloud, targeting roughly 45–60 seconds. Choose varied life situations: protection, forgiveness, healing, hope, gratitude, peace, anxiety, family, resilience and strength. Avoid repeating recent topics or openings when history is available. Never invent Bible quotations or references. Be compassionate and clear without sensational claims. Match the prayer's emotion to the selected visuals and music.
