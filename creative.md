# Too Raw Media — Creative Direction

## Prayer
Create original, heartfelt Christian prayers around varied life situations, including protection, forgiveness, healing, gratitude, hope, strength, peace and resilience. Do not fabricate Bible quotes or references. Avoid repetitive intros and topics.

## Images
Use @too.raw.media as the user's visual reference: dramatic cinematic Christian storytelling, not generic stock photography. Generate vertical 9:16, realistic-looking digital artwork with emotional characters, kneeling prayer, biblical settings when relevant, heavenly golden light, storms, clouds, striking shadows and warm highlights. Leave safe room for captions. Avoid text, watermarks and logos in source assets.

Examples: protection = person under heavenly light amid a storm; forgiveness = person kneeling in prayer; healing = comforting light; strength = warrior praying; gratitude = sunrise.

When asked to 'generate assets', consult the newest accessible sanitized asset index, identify missing or overused themes, create varied images, and provide clear filenames plus 3–5 tags. If the index isn't accessible, explain that briefly and use these defaults without demanding another creative brief.

## Audio and video
Use varied, fitting background music only when its TikTok publishing rights are recorded as approved. Make readable word-synced captions within safe margins. Quality check a 9:16 H.264 MP4, sound balance and visible captions before any publication.
